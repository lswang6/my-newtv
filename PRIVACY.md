# 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy — 我的电视 NewMyTV (iOS · iPadOS · tvOS · macOS)

最后更新 / Last updated: 2026-09-29

## 中文

「我的电视」不收集、不存储、也不向开发者发送任何个人信息。

- 没有账号、广告、统计分析或崩溃上报 SDK。
- 你添加的播放列表链接、最近观看的频道只保存在本机（系统 UserDefaults），不会上传。
- 播放时，App 会直接连接你在播放列表中提供的媒体服务器和台标图片地址。这些第三方服务器可能按其自身政策记录你的 IP 地址等访问信息，与本 App 开发者无关。
- 删除 App 即可清除所有本地数据。

联系方式：lswang6@gmail.com · https://github.com/lswang6/my-newtv/issues

## English

NewMyTV does not collect, store, or send any personal data to the developer.

- No accounts, advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting SDKs.
- The playlist link you add and your last-watched channel are stored only on your device (UserDefaults) and are never uploaded.
- During playback the app connects directly to the media servers and logo URLs listed in the playlist you provide. Those third-party servers may log your IP address and request details under their own policies; the developer has no access to that data.
- Deleting the app removes all local data.

Contact: lswang6@gmail.com · https://github.com/lswang6/my-newtv/issues
